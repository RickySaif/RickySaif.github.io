## The CEO's problem

You know something is missing. Sales are flat, or a competitor is winning deals you used to win. But "something is missing" is not a plan.

Most gap analyses die in a slide deck. Why? Because they list everything, and a list of everything has no first step.

## What the AI does

The AI collects the current picture from what you give it: your offer, your process, your website, your numbers. It does the same for the target, which can be your own goal for next year or a competitor that is doing better.

Then it puts the two side by side, names each gap, and ranks the gaps by **impact on profit** and **effort to close**.

## What you get

- A short list of gaps, ordered by what pays back first.
- The reasoning behind each one, so you can challenge it.
- A first step you can start on Monday.

The AI does the reading and the comparing. You do the judging, because only you know which gaps you are willing to close.
