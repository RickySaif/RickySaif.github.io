## The CEO's problem

Your competitor launched a new offer last month. It seems to work.

You want to test the same idea. Then the quote arrives: six weeks and a budget you have to defend to the board. By the time the page is live, the window has closed.

> "I don't need a perfect website. I need to know if anyone will buy."

## What the AI does

The free tool drives a real browser (Playwright) to walk through the competitor's page the way a visitor would. It records the structure: what comes first, which promise sits above the fold, how the offer is framed, where the buttons lead.

Then AI rebuilds **your own version** from that map, with your brand, your words and your offer. You get a working page you can put live the same week.

## What you get

- A live, testable page for your idea, in days instead of weeks.
- A clear map of how the competitor sells, so you can decide where to differ.
- A decision based on real visitors and real sign-ups, not on a meeting.

If nobody buys, you have lost a few days. Not a quarter.

## How we do it responsibly

We study what a competitor has proven: the structure, the flow, the order of the argument. We never copy their code, text, images or brand. Your page uses your words and your assets.

That is not only the legal line. It is also the business line, because a copy teaches you nothing about your own customers.
