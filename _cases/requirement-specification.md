## The CEO's problem

You explained what you wanted in a meeting. The vendor nodded. Three months later you received something else, and every fix is now a paid "change request".

Was the vendor wrong? Often not. **Nobody wrote down what "done" means.**

Building software without a spec is like building a house from a conversation. Everyone remembers the conversation differently.

## What the AI does

You give the AI what you already have: meeting notes, WhatsApp threads, a voice memo recorded in the car. It turns them into one structured specification: goals, users, features, rules, and what is out of scope.

Then it does the part people skip. It lists the **open questions**, the places where your notes contradict each other or say nothing at all.

## What you get

- One document that you, your team and the vendor read the same way.
- Quotes you can compare, because every vendor is quoting the same thing.
- Fewer change requests, because the gaps were closed before the work started.

You still make every decision. The AI only makes sure none of them is left implicit.
